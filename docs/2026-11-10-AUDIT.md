## Bluetooth

The newer authentication/notification order from r172 is preserved. Two defects
from the earlier audit remain in upstream main and are fixed here:

1. Awaiting a native write before observing its reply timeout can leave connect
   hung and produce an unhandled rejection. The deadline now remains observed
   while the write is pending.
2. Browser method presence does not indicate which GATT write modes a device
   supports. The shared Peak writer now respects characteristic properties.

`test-lorax-connection.js` was updated for r172's read-only firmware query. The
unpatched current client reproduced the hung-write failure. The patched client
passes success, silence, hung write, early reply, failed write and acknowledged-
only transport cases. These are simulated GATT tests; no physical Peak success
is claimed. Other native setup stages still rely on browser promises completing.

## Wizard: work in progress, not a crowd replacement

The relevant close reference is `tools/video-frames/reference/hall-01-f00079.png`:
a hooded dark-fantasy wizard with a gaunt face, bright eyes, angular hands and
strongly shaded blue-black fabric. The rejected smooth pointed-hat prototype
missed that target.

`tools/assets/ashen-wizard.py` starts a different asset in Blender: fused sculpt
volumes, a face opening in the hood, sculpted sockets/nose/cheeks, continuous
beard and robe surfaces, and fingers attached to the hands. Its GLB is a rest
mesh with vertex colours; the eye insets are separate islands within the mesh.
The source and three renders are included for review and further modelling.
The current draft is **not reference-quality or animation-ready**. Surface paint
needs more controlled placement, hand anatomy and topology need close review,
and the rig, weights, staff interaction and seated garment correction remain
to be built. No studio button or live NPC replacement is shipped for this draft.

Do not mistake a fused mesh or a passing export for those remaining acceptance
criteria. Preserve the current map and crowd until one complete character
passes visual review in standing, walking and seated states.
